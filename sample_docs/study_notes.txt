Study notes: Reinforcement Learning

Sample-average methods weight every past reward equally. They work well when
the problem is stationary (reward probabilities never change).

Constant step-size methods (alpha = 0.1, for example) weight recent rewards
more heavily. They track nonstationary problems better because old rewards
fade out exponentially.

Upper-Confidence-Bound (UCB) action selection adds an exploration bonus to
actions that have been tried less often, instead of exploring at random like
epsilon-greedy.
